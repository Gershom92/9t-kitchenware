Choosing glassware and bar accessories

Choose glassware to suit the drinks you serve: flutes for sparkling drinks, stemmed glasses for wine, and tumblers for spirits or cocktails. Check capacity and the exact number of glasses included before ordering. For ice buckets, consider available serving space, bottle sizes and whether you need a lid or tongs.
